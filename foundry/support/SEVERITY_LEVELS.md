# Severity Levels

| Severity | Meaning | Typical Handling |
|---|---|---|
| S0 | Production outage, data exposure, or destructive behavior | Stop and escalate for Dobromir approval. |
| S1 | Major broken workflow or security/privacy risk | Escalate before fix unless clearly internal and approved. |
| S2 | Broken internal workflow, build, typecheck, lint, or documented path | Codex may fix inside approved scope. |
| S3 | Documentation issue, typo, broken link, low-risk internal bug | Codex may fix inside approved scope. |
| S4 | Improvement or cleanup | Plan through normal Linear workflow. |
