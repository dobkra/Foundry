# Planning Pack Selection Guide

Use planning packs progressively. Add only the packs needed to clarify, approve, and prepare the work.

## Selection Rules

- Tiny documentation or bugfix work: Basic Staging Packet only.
- Unclear ideas: Discovery Pack.
- System/repo/module decisions: Architecture Pack.
- Business workflows/service model: Business Functions Pack.
- Success criteria/KPIs: Goals and Outcomes Pack.
- UI/workflow planning: UI / Workflow Sprint Pack.
- Linear-ready work: Issue Breakdown Pack after approval.

## Combination Rules

- Packs can be combined.
- Packs should be added progressively.
- Do not overbuild small work.
- Do not create Linear issues before approved issue breakdown.
- Do not start Codex execution before execution readiness allows it.

## Progressive Complexity

Simple work should stay simple. Larger or higher-risk work can use additional packs when more clarity is needed before implementation.

## Approval Boundary

Planning packs do not replace Launcher review gates. Dobromir remains required for direction/authority decisions.
